Thesis Path

I am an Aerospace Engineer and graduate student dedicated to the future of advanced aerial systems, with a core focus on Autonomy, Program Management, and Aerodynamics. My passion lies in transitioning theoretical design into high-fidelity tested hardware, and I am currently applying this focus to my M.S. thesis on next-generation Counter-UAS (C-UAS) technology.

Featured Work

Counter-UAS Master Thesis

Developed a vision-based pipeline to extract position, velocity, attitude, and angular rates from video feeds of a non-communicating drone to estimate current states and to predict future states.

Project VECTOR

This is a simulation framework for predicting flight paths of rogue or non-communicating Unmanned Aerial Systems (UASs) operating below a 50-meter city roofline.

Fixed-Wing UAS

Flight Test Manufacturing

Engineered and flight-tested a 25-lb autonomous fixed-wing aircraft. Designed in SolidWorks, manufactured using composite materials, and integrated a flight controller for autonomous waypoint navigation.

Configurable Thrust Stand

Thrust Test

Developed a custom Arduino load-cell testing apparatus for electric RC motors, diagnosing inefficiencies in testing methods and achieving total data uncertainty under 3%.
