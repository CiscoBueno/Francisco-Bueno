Thesis Path

I am an Aerospace Engineer and graduate student dedicated to the future of advanced aerial systems, with a core focus on Autonomy, Program Management, and Aerodynamics. My passion lies in transitioning theoretical design into high-fidelity tested hardware, and I am currently applying this focus to my M.S. thesis on next-generation Counter-UAS (C-UAS) technology.

Vision-Based 6D Pose Estimation (VECTOR)

Developed a vision-based pipeline utilizing Meta's Segment Anything Model 2 and Lucas-Kanade Optical Flow to extract position, attitude, and angular rates from video feeds. Verified against onboard Pixhawk GNSS/INS telemetry.

Group II Fixed-Wing UAS Development

Engineered and flight-tested a 25-lb autonomous fixed-wing aircraft. Designed 450+ parts in SolidWorks, manufactured using composite materials, and integrated Pixhawk systems for autonomous waypoint navigation.

Configurable Propulsion Thrust Stand

Developed a custom C++/Arduino load-cell testing apparatus for electric RC motors, diagnosing inefficiencies in testing methods and achieving total data uncertainty under 3%.
